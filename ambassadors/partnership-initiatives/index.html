How we partner

A few ways we work together.

Every partnership is built around a clear, real outcome, not just a logo swap.

Model 1

University & hub partnerships

Co-running training labs and student dev sprints on campus.

  • Dedicated CodeCave leads
  • Curriculum built together
  • Student portfolio reviews
Ideal for CS depts and hubs
Model 2

Hackathons & builder sprints

Co-hosting events like Hackathon 27 with shared problem tracks.

  • Joint event planning
  • Shared mentorship pool
  • Judging and demo day
Ideal for tech communities
Model 3

Tooling & platform partners

Providing credits, tools or infrastructure to our students and cohorts.

  • Free or discounted access
  • Co-branded workshops
  • Usage feedback loop
Ideal for dev tool companies
Model 4

Sponsorship & grants

  • Transparent fund use
  • Public recognition
  • Impact reporting
